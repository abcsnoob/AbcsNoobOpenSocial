# Contributor License Policy

**Abc's Noob Open Social**

## 1. Purpose

This policy exists to ensure that:

* The project **remains Free Software** under the **GNU General Public License v3 (GPLv3)**
* **No individual, organization, government, or Big Tech company** can:

  * take control of the project
  * close the source code
  * introduce backdoors
  * impose legal or technical control over users
* The original developer **has no special authority** over contributors or users

---

## 3. No Contributor License Agreement (No CLA)

This project **does NOT use a Contributor License Agreement (CLA)**.

That means:

* ❌ No copyright transfer to the original developer
* ❌ No relicensing to proprietary or closed licenses
* ❌ No dual-licensing
* ❌ No exclusive commercial control

👉 Contributors **retain copyright** over their contributions
👉 The community receives **perpetual rights** under GPLv3

---

## 4. Contributor Rights

By contributing, you:

* Retain ownership of your original work
* Grant everyone the right to:

  * use
  * study
  * modify
  * distribute
  * fork
    under the terms of **GPLv3**
* Do **not** grant anyone the right to:

  * add backdoors
  * restrict users
  * collect user data secretly
  * control or disable other nodes

---

## 5. Contributor Responsibilities

By submitting a contribution, you affirm that:

1. You are the original author, or you have the legal right to submit the work
2. Your contribution does **not** violate:

   * third-party copyrights
   * restrictive patents
3. Your contribution does **not contain**:

   * backdoors
   * hidden tracking mechanisms
   * centralized control logic
   * kill switches
4. Any **intentional malicious contribution** may result in permanent public rejection and disclosure

---

## 6. Security and Backdoor Policy

* There is **no absolute trusted maintainer**
* All security-sensitive changes must be:

  * transparent
  * well-documented
  * publicly reviewable
* **Security through transparency**, not blind trust

---

## 7. Patents

If you own or control patents that are relevant to your contribution:

* You automatically grant a **worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive patent license**
  to all users, consistent with GPLv3
* You may **not**:

  * sue users
  * demand patent royalties
  * use patents as legal leverage against the project or its users

---

## 8. No Central Authority

* The original developer has **no power** to:

  * revoke licenses
  * reset the network
  * access user data
  * disable nodes
* The project has **no central server** to seize, shut down, or control

---

## 9. No Password Recovery

* Lost keys mean lost access
* There is no centralized recovery
* There are no backdoors for developers, administrators, or governments

👉 This is a **feature**, not a bug.

---
